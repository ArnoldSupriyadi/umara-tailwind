Fueling Excellence: Umara Catering at the Gymnastics World Championships

In the world of elite sports, precision and performance are everything. Umara Mitra Kulina (Umara Group) is incredibly proud to have been chosen as the official catering partner for the prestigious Gymnastics World Championships, ensuring that world-class athletes and VIP guests experienced the finest hospitality Indonesia has to offer.

Serving on an international stage requires more than just culinary skill; it demands strict adherence to nutritional standards and impeccable hygiene. Umara rose to the challenge by curating menus that were not only delicious but also perfectly balanced to meet the dietary needs of high-performance gymnasts from around the globe.

Exclusive VVIP Hospitality

Beyond the athletes' dining, Umara also managed the VVIP and VIP Lounges. We transformed these spaces into exclusive culinary havens, offering a premium buffet spread featuring a fusion of international cuisines and authentic Indonesian delicacies. Our goal was to provide a comforting and luxurious experience for dignitaries, judges, and officials amidst the high-energy atmosphere of the competition.

This event stands as a testament to Umara’s capability to handle large-scale international events with professional excellence. From the kitchen to the service floor, our team worked tirelessly to deliver "The Taste of Hospitality," proving once again that Umara is the trusted partner for world-class occasions.
