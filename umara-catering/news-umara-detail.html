IKASTARA IHFT 2025: Alumni Futsal Tournament & Reunion

IKASTARA once again brought the spirit of unity to life through the IHFT – Ikastara Happy Futsal Tournament 2025, an annual futsal competition that serves as a platform for alumni camaraderie, sportsmanship, and pride among the graduates of SMA Taruna Nusantara.

Held in a warm and energetic atmosphere, IHFT 2025 featured exciting matches from various alumni batches—from senior graduates to the newest generations. Each team competed with strong motivation, carrying the spirit of “Tidar Pride” and upholding the values of leadership, discipline, and friendship that have been instilled since their school days.

More than just a futsal tournament, IHFT 2025 became a grand reunion moment, reconnecting alumni from diverse professions and regions. Amid their busy lives, this event provided the space to share stories, strengthen alumni networks, and celebrate togetherness through a positive and uplifting sports environment.

With enthusiastic participation, supportive spectators, and a tournament filled with sportsmanship, IHFT 2025 stands as a testament that the IKASTARA family remains solid, active, and committed to maintaining unity across generations. This event is expected to further inspire collaborative alumni activities in the future.
