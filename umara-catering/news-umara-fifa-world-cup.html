Chosen for Excellence: Umara Catering Serves VVIPs at FIFA World Cup 2026 Event

The global spotlight turned to Jakarta on January 22, 2026, as the Jakarta Convention Center (JCC) hosted a prestigious official event for the FIFA World Cup 2026. In an event demanding the highest standards of security, service, and culinary excellence, Umara Catering was entrusted with the privilege of serving the VVIP guests in the exclusive lounge.

Serving the Nation's Leaders

We were honored to welcome distinguished state officials and international dignitaries, including the Minister of SOEs and Chairman of PSSI, Mr. Erick Thohir, and the Governor of Jakarta, Mr. Pramono Anung. Catering for state officials requires flawless execution. Our team delivered a sophisticated dining experience that blended Indonesia's rich culinary heritage with the refined elegance expected on the world stage.

Gastronomic Diplomacy in the VIP Room

Inside the VIP Room, Umara Catering presented a curated menu designed to showcase the best of Indonesian flavors. From exquisite appetizers to main courses prepared with premium local ingredients, every dish was a testament to our commitment to quality. Being chosen for this FIFA event reaffirms Umara Catering's position as a trusted partner for high-profile government and international events in Indonesia.
