Umara Group Expands Catering Services Across Indonesia

By Umara Group Communications • 5 min read

Umara Group's premium catering services expanding to new cities across Indonesia

We're excited to announce the expansion of our catering services to new cities across Indonesia, bringing our exceptional culinary experience to more communities nationwide.

This strategic expansion represents a significant milestone in Umara Group's commitment to delivering premium food services throughout the archipelago. Our new locations will feature the same high-quality standards and innovative menu offerings that have made us a trusted name in the catering industry.

Expanding Our Reach

The expansion includes new service areas in major cities including Surabaya, Medan, Makassar, and Bandung. Each location will be equipped with state-of-the-art kitchen facilities and staffed by experienced culinary professionals trained in our signature cooking methods.

"This expansion allows us to bring our passion for exceptional food and service to more Indonesian families and businesses. We're committed to maintaining our high standards while adapting to local tastes and preferences."

— CEO, Umara Group

What This Means for Our Customers

  • Extended service coverage across major Indonesian cities
  • Faster delivery times and improved logistics
  • Local menu adaptations featuring regional specialties
  • Enhanced customer support with local teams
  • Competitive pricing with maintained quality standards

Our expansion strategy focuses on sustainable growth while maintaining the quality and service excellence that our customers expect. Each new location undergoes rigorous quality control processes and staff training programs to ensure consistency across all our operations.

Looking Forward

This expansion is just the beginning of our ambitious growth plans for 2024. We're also exploring opportunities in the digital space, including enhanced online ordering systems and mobile applications to make our services more accessible to customers across Indonesia.
