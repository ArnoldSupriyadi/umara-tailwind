Caring for the Village: CSR Program in Sekongkang Atas

On July 18, 2025, our team carried out a simple yet meaningful CSR activity in Sekongkang Atas Village as part of our Caring for the Village program.

We shared 7 cartons of Sari Kacang Hijau and 8 cartons of Teh Kotak, with a total value of Rp 1,495,000. While it may seem like a small gesture, we believe these healthy drinks will bring warmth, energy, and a little happiness to the community.

Why It Matters

  • Supports local communities with nutritious and refreshing drinks
  • Strengthens the bond between Umara Group and the village
  • Shows that even small actions can create positive impact

This initiative is more than just giving—it’s about building connections and showing that we care. Through small steps like this, we hope to support local communities and inspire more kindness along the way.

"Together, let’s keep creating a better future—one small act of kindness at a time." — Umara Group CSR Team

This is just one of many CSR activities we plan to continue, focusing on education, health, and sustainable community empowerment. We believe small contributions can lead to meaningful change.
