Umara Catering at Honda Culture Indonesia

Honda Culture Indonesia made a vibrant return, bringing together Honda enthusiasts from across the country. With the spirit of “Menyala Sepanjang Masa”, the event blended automotive passion, urban lifestyle, and community creativity.

Iconic Honda models from various generations lined the venue, complemented by urban culture showcases and exciting community activities that enriched this year’s experience even more.

Adding to the celebration, Umara Catering participated with a special booth serving delicious ready-to-enjoy meals and signature hospitality. Visitors enjoyed flavorful dishes while exploring different zones and programs across the Honda community event.

This collaboration perfectly showcases how automotive culture, lifestyle, and culinary experiences can come together to create a dynamic and memorable event for all attendees.
