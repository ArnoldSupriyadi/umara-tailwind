Expanding Horizons: New Production Facility in Karawang

October 24, 2024 4 min read

The new state-of-the-art facility in Karawang Industrial Estate

To meet the continuously growing demand for high-quality industrial catering services in West Java, PT Umara Mitra Kulina is proud to announce the official opening of our newest central kitchen facility in the Karawang International Industrial City (KIIC).

This strategic expansion marks a significant milestone in our journey to become Indonesia's most reliable food service partner. With this new facility, we are not just increasing our capacity; we are elevating the standards of industrial catering through advanced technology and rigorous safety protocols.

Boosting Capacity & Efficiency

The new 2,500 square meter facility is equipped with modern automated cooking lines and a blast-chilling system that ensures food safety and freshness. This infrastructure upgrade allows us to:

  • Increase daily production capacity by 200%, reaching up to 15,000 portions per shift.
  • Reduce delivery lead times to major industrial zones in Karawang and Cikarang by 40%.
  • Implement a zero-waste water management system aligned with our sustainability goals.

"Our investment in this facility reflects our confidence in the Indonesian manufacturing sector. We are here to support our clients' growth by ensuring their workforce is fueled with nutritious, safe, and delicious meals every single day."

Aditya Mufti Arifin - CEO, Umara Group

Commitment to Quality

Alongside the physical expansion, we have also upgraded our Quality Management System. The new facility is fully compliant with ISO 22000:2018 standards and Halal Assurance System requirements.

We invite our partners and potential clients to schedule a visit to our new kitchen and witness firsthand how we prepare thousands of meals with the care and precision of a fine-dining establishment.
