Serving Joy at Chemco Funday 2025: Umara Supports Massive Annual Gathering at KIM Karawang

December 14, 2025 3 min read

The vibrant atmosphere of Chemco Funday 2025 at KIM Karawang factory grounds.

Large-scale events require precision, energy, and excellent food. On December 14, 2025, Umara Mitra Kulina had the distinct privilege of being the trusted catering partner for our esteemed client, PT Chemco Harapan Nusantara, during their grand annual event.

Held at the sprawling Chemco factory grounds in the Mitra Karawang Industrial Estate (KIM), this year’s gathering was a monumental occasion. It marked a massive reunion that brought together thousands of employees from two major production sites: Chemco Karawang and Chemco Cikarang.

Fueling the Spirit of the Event

At Umara Mitra Kulina, we believe that great food is the heart of every successful gathering. Managing the catering for a massive industrial event like Chemco Funday requires logistical excellence and a commitment to hygiene and taste.

We are proud to have supported Chemco Indonesia in making this event a seamless and memorable experience. We congratulate the entire Chemco family on a successful 2025 gathering and look forward to serving more moments of joy in the future.
