QC Piping & Mechanic — PT Rasa Nusantara Baru

Production & Manufacturing • Jakarta • On-site

Role Summary

Responsible for assuring quality of piping, mechanical assembly, and equipment installation according to SOP and safety standards across production lines.

Requirements

  • Diploma/Bachelor in Mechanical/Industrial Engineering or related field.
  • 2–4 years experience in piping QC, fabrication, or mechanical assembly.
  • Strong understanding of P&ID, isometric drawings, tolerances, and welding standards.
  • Hands-on inspection skills (visual, dimension, fit-up) & basic NDT familiarity.
  • Knowledge of GMP, HACCP, and safety practices (preferred for F&B manufacturing).
  • Detail-oriented, able to create QC reports & collaborate with production teams.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary & performance bonus.
  • BPJS Kesehatan & Ketenagakerjaan.
  • Meal allowance & shift allowance (if applicable).
  • Training & career development opportunities.
