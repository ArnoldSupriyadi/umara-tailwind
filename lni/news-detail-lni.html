Distribution Expansion to New Retail Partners

15 November 2025

Laukita continues to grow across major retailers, bringing premium ready-to-eat meals closer to customers. This milestone is driven by strong demand for convenient, high-quality foods crafted with authentic ingredients.

With an expanding distribution network, our products are now more accessible across leading supermarkets and specialty stores. We maintain strict quality standards to ensure taste, freshness, and safety throughout the supply chain.

What This Means for Customers

Customers can expect broader availability, faster restocks, and curated selections tailored to local preferences. We are committed to delivering consistency and culinary enjoyment with every pack.

Key Points

  • Expanded presence across partner retailers
  • Continuous quality checks and cold-chain integrity
  • Introduction of new menu items based on feedback
