Rasa Umara x Wardah: Bazar Fashion & Beauty Experience 2025

Rasa Umara collaborates with Wardah Beauty to present a special event titled “Explore the Rasa Umara Fashion Bazaar”—a celebration of Rasa Umara’s Anniversary filled with inspiration and personalized beauty experiences.

At this event, guests can enjoy a series of activities, from the fashion bazaar to Wardah’s Personal Analyzed session, helping each visitor discover the beauty products that best suit their skin needs.

In addition, various special gifts, entertainment, and fun activities are available throughout the day, creating a warm, joyful, and empowering celebration for everyone.

This Rasa Umara x Wardah collaboration invites every guest to glow and grow together, embracing beauty and confidence in their best version.
