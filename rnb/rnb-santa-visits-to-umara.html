A Magical Christmas: Santa Surprises Guests at Umara House & Lumpang Emas Signature

Christmas is a season of giving, sharing, and creating beautiful memories with loved ones. To celebrate this joyous occasion, Umara Group brought a special touch of magic to its beloved dining establishments. On December 24 and 25, 2025, guests dining at Umara House (Benhil) and Lumpang Emas Signature (Prapanca) were treated to a delightful holiday surprise.

Amidst the warm glow of Christmas decorations and the aroma of our signature dishes, a very special guest made an appearance. Santa Claus himself walked through the dining halls, greeting families, taking photos, and spreading cheer to everyone present.

The highlight of the event was the Gift Giving Moment. Santa approached each table to personally deliver special Christmas gifts to our dining guests. The smiles on the faces of children and adults alike lit up the room, turning a delicious dinner into an unforgettable experience.

"We wanted to do more than just serve great food; we wanted to create a moment of happiness," said the restaurant management. This event reflects Umara Group's commitment to providing not just culinary excellence, but also a warm, family-friendly atmosphere where every guest feels cherished.
