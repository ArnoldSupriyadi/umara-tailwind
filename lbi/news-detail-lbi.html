Laukita Bersama Indonesia Showcases Food Manufacturing at SIAL InterFood 2025

PT Laukita Bersama Indonesia (LBI) participated in SIAL InterFood 2025, one of Indonesia’s leading food and beverage exhibitions. This participation serves as an important step for LBI to introduce its advanced frozen and retort food manufacturing capabilities to the public, industry players, and potential partners.

Established in 2018, LBI has become a trusted toll manufacturing and white label partner, delivering high-quality frozen, retort, and pasteurized food solutions. With state-of-the-art facilities and modern safety standards, LBI supports the entire production lifecycle—from concept to completion.

"LBI’s presence at SIAL InterFood reinforces its commitment to delivering future-ready food manufacturing solutions that emphasize quality, efficiency, and innovation."

Through this exhibition, LBI aims to expand collaboration opportunities with F&B brands, hotels, restaurants, cafés, and FMCG companies seeking a reliable and innovative manufacturing partner capable of supporting business scalability.
